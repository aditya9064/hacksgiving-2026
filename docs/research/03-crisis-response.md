# 03 — Alternative Crisis Response Programs

ComForce's work overlaps with mental-health crises, accidents, fires, and missing persons. These programs show how non-police responders coordinate, and why they survive or collapse.

| Program | Where | Scale & cost | Worked ✅ | Went wrong ❌ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CAHOOTS** | Eugene, OR (since 1989) | About 24,000 calls in 2019; only 311 needed police backup. About $2M/yr, roughly 2% of police budgets. Saved Eugene PD about $2.2M/yr. | A 30-year proof that civilian responders work | **Ended in Eugene in April 2025.** The city contract covered only about 40% of operating costs. The value was real, but **it was never captured in a way that secured funding.** |
| **STAR** | Denver | 748 calls in the 2020 pilot. About $1.4M in 2021. | −34% low-level crime in STAR precincts. About $151 per offense averted vs. $646 for police. | Only 2.8% of calls were eligible, so scope was narrow. **42% of dispatches came from 911 call-takers**, which made the dispatch relationship the real front door. |
| **CART** | Milwaukee | Over 4,500 encounters in 2025. 41% stabilized on scene, about 8% emergency detentions. | Seven uses of force in 12 years | Police-led, and data stays inside MPD and the county. Grassroots groups have no shared view for follow-up. |
| **County Crisis Line** | Milwaukee (414-257-7222) | Over 22,000 calls and 8,200 mobile responses in 2025 | Large reach, 18% youth | Siloed from community organizations |

## Takeaways for us

1. **CAHOOTS is the cautionary tale for our pitch.** A program that saved its city $2.2M a year still lost funding because nobody packaged the evidence. *ComForce says it operates "on minimum funding."* The impact layer of our system addresses exactly this risk.
2. **The front door decides everything** (STAR). We must make it trivially easy for residents, partners, and dispatchers to hand a request to ComForce.
3. **Stay out of police data flows.** Milwaukee's crisis data is police- and county-held. ComForce's trust comes from *not* being police (see the [Milwaukee context](06-milwaukee-context.md)).
