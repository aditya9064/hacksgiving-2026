## Other MSOE AI Club competitions

- **Innovation Labs Fall 2025 (Xorbix):** rewarded evaluation rigor and real deployment to mobile, web, and VR.
- **Innovation Labs Spring 2026:** 1st place had deep domain knowledge (clinical coding). Special award for "Best Deployment."

## Winning patterns

1. **End-to-end, working, polished.** No "imagine if" slides.
2. **Responsible AI as engineering**, not as a disclaimer slide (Discovery Mate's hallucination control).
3. **Domain depth.** Speak the partner's language.
4. **A real deployment** beats a localhost demo.
5. **A memorable physical or live moment** (Yarvis's Bluetooth beacons).
