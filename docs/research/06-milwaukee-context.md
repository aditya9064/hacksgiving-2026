# 06 — Milwaukee Context, Judges & Open Data

## ComForce MKE in the ecosystem

- Partnered with the City's **Department of Community Wellness and Safety (DCWS)** for about 4 years, as a branch of **MKE Promise Keepers**. Vaun Mayes: *"We've been operating on minimum funding."* Residents call ComForce directly, and the team is sometimes on scene **before first responders**. ([Carvd N Stone, sponsored](https://carvdnstone.com/comforce-mke-team-unified-and-milwaukees-critical-response-team-continue-making-a-difference-in-communities-impacted-by-violence-through-city-partnership/))
- Peer organizations: **Team Unified** (South Side, Pastor Marty Calderon) and the **DCWS Critical Response Team** (retaliation, gang conflict, missing persons).
- 2026: 85 homicides as of October (MPD via the article above). The DCWS goal is to stay under 100.
- Other actors: 414LIFE, Blueprint for Peace, Sojourner Family Peace Center, County BHS crisis line, CART, IMPACT 211.
- See [the ComForce profile](../challenge/comforce-mke-profile.md) for divisions and services.
