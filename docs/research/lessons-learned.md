# Consolidated Lessons Learned

Twelve lessons from about 20 programs and platforms. Each one maps to a design decision.

| # | Lesson | Evidence | Design decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Proving impact is survival.** Good programs die without packaged evidence. | CAHOOTS defunded in Eugene in 2025 despite saving about $2.2M/yr. 414LIFE's null Phase 1 result. ComForce "on minimum funding." | An auto-generated, auditable **impact report** is a first-class feature, not an afterthought |
| 2 | **Referrals vanish into a black hole** unless "closed" is defined | Unite Us/NCCARE360 complaints; vendor analyses | A fixed status vocabulary, with *who delivered* and *when* |
| 3 | **Provider self-reporting is weak evidence** | Inconsistent closure definitions everywhere | **Resident-verified outcomes** via a follow-up text (unique to us) |
| 4 | **Duplicate assignment wastes responders** | NCCARE360; Ushahidi duplicates | **Claim-to-own**: one request has one owner |
| 5 | **Visibility without action harms people** | Ushahidi Haiti: texts mapped, never answered | Every request gets an acknowledgment and a final reply to the requester |
| 6 | **New portals don't get adopted** | Free e-referral platform: about 8 referrals a month after 4 years | Meet people in **SMS/chat**. The web is only for coordinators. |
| 7 | **The tool must pay off for one org alone** | The adoption study's "thin network" | Useful to ComForce on day one. Partners are optional. |
| 8 | **Humans beat algorithms at finding who needs help** | READI: outreach referrals outperformed ML | AI *structures* information. Humans *decide*. No risk scores for people. |
| 9 | **Location-level anonymized data is enough** | Cardiff Model | Maps show needs by neighborhood, never individuals |
| 10 | **Track activity and fidelity, not just citywide outcomes** | Oakland decay; Baltimore site variance | Dashboards for response times, follow-ups, and capacity per program |
| 11 | **Shared dashboards build partner trust** | LA GRYD | Partners see their own contributions (the "who delivered" credit) |
| 12 | **Cost and lock-in exclude grassroots orgs** | findhelp $5.4k–$75k/yr; Azure-based hubs | Open source, free tier, under $25/mo, one-click export |
