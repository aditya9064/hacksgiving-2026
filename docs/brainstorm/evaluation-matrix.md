## Reading the matrix

- No single feature beats the combined loop. The risk of the combination is **feasibility**, so scope must be ruthless (see [recommended-concept.md](recommended-concept.md)).
- **E (impact report) is the highest-scoring single feature.** It is where the CAHOOTS lesson lands, and it must be the climax of the demo.
- **D (text-to-case AI)** carries the most "wow" per hour of effort and is the AI Club judge's hook.
- **H (training simulator)** is the most *unique*. It is a good stretch module if time allows.
